2. Purpose, audience and value

Ronpark Study is an educational study-management app for learners preparing
for examinations, including Korean coast guard recruitment examinations.
It brings study planning, study-time tracking, progress information and
communication with instructors into one service. Depending on the learner's
course type, the app also provides attendance and outing management, exam
results, subject discussion boards, or interview-preparation tools.

The app supports three general learner experiences: offline students,
online managed-class students, and internet-course students. These experiences expose different menus
according to the learner's enrollment; a missing menu can therefore reflect
the account's course type.

All app features are provided free of charge. There are no paid app features
that the reviewer must purchase to complete the review.
